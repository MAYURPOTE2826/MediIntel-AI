
# 🏥 MediIntel AI

**MediIntel AI** is an AI-powered Medical Report Intelligence Platform designed to help people understand complex medical reports in a simple, multilingual, and patient-friendly way.

The platform supports medical documents such as ECG reports, X-rays, CT scans, MRI scans, ultrasound reports, blood test reports, prescriptions, and other clinical documents. Using Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, OCR, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Medical Language Models, MediIntel AI extracts meaningful information and presents it in an easy-to-understand format.

## 🚀 Vision

To empower every patient to understand their medical reports before consulting a healthcare professional.

## 🎯 Mission

Build an intelligent healthcare platform that bridges the communication gap between medical reports and patients while supporting—not replacing—qualified healthcare professionals.

## ⚠ Disclaimer

MediIntel AI is designed to assist users in understanding medical reports. It does **not** provide a final medical diagnosis, prescribe medications, or replace licensed healthcare professionals. Users should always consult a qualified doctor for medical decisions.
